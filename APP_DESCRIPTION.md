## Description (max 4000 characters)

Keep every account safe with Authenticator — the all-in-one security app that
combines two-factor authentication, a smart password toolkit, and a private
browser, all protected behind a passcode or Face ID.

Your data never leaves your iPhone. Secrets are stored locally and encrypted, so
you stay in complete control.

ONE PLACE FOR ALL YOUR 2FA CODES
- Add accounts in seconds by scanning a QR code or entering a key manually
- Time-based one-time codes (TOTP) that rotate every 30 seconds, just like
  Google Authenticator
- A live countdown ring so you always know how long a code stays valid
- One-tap copy and instant search across all your accounts

STRONG PASSWORDS, INSTANTLY
- Generate unique, hard-to-crack passwords in a single tap
- Store and organize your logins securely, right on your device
- Stop reusing weak passwords across your accounts

PRIVATE BROWSING
- Browse without leaving a trace — sessions clear automatically on exit
- No history and no tracking left behind

SECURITY YOU CAN TRUST
- Lock the app with a passcode or Face ID
- Everything is stored locally and encrypted — nothing is uploaded or shared
- Works fully offline

WHY AUTHENTICATOR?
- Clean, fast, and effortless to use
- Unlimited 2FA accounts
- No ads, no clutter — just security

Download Authenticator today and take control of your digital security.

SUBSCRIPTION & PRIVACY
Authenticator offers optional subscriptions (weekly, monthly, or yearly) with a
free trial to unlock premium features. Payment is charged to your Apple ID
account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless
canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage
or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are available inside the app.
